Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

PartyCards is a local party card game. The app does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Information You Enter

Player names entered in setup are used only on your device during the current game session. They are not sent to PartyCards or any third party.

Storage

The app may cache game files on your device so the game loads faster and can continue working more smoothly. This cache does not identify you.

Analytics and Tracking

The current app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, login systems, payments, location access, contacts access, camera access, or microphone access.

Children

The app is intended for social party gameplay. Store age ratings should be completed honestly based on the game content and any alcohol-related prompts in the selected decks.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the PartyCards owner through the support email listed on the app store page.
